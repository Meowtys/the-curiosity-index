## Problem & Context

Running university-wide student government elections through a general-purpose Learning Management System (LMS) forces election commissioners into fragile workarounds: manual course enrollments per college, awkward quiz-style ballot layouts on mobile phones, and slow spreadsheet exports when verifying turnout.

Started in **2024** as a team prototype for **Silliman University Student Government (SUSG)** elections and rebuilt and expanded in **2026**, the **SUSG Voting System** is a purpose-built web platform where students cast verified ballots from their phone or laptop and the Commission on Elections (**Comelec**) operates the entire election lifecycle from a unified administrative dashboard.

## Architecture & Rebuild Highlights

### Server-Enforced College Ballot Scoping

University elections combine campus-wide executive races (President and Vice President) with college-specific representative seats where each academic unit elects a distinct quota of representatives.

- **Zero Client-Side Trust:** Ballot eligibility and seat limits are enforced strictly on the server in **PHP 8.4** and **MariaDB** based on the authenticated voter's college affiliation.
- **Single-Submission Integrity:** Database constraints and transactional ballot recording ensure a student can cast exactly one ballot per election while preserving ballot secrecy from administrative tampering.

### Comelec Administration & Atomic Live Results Caching

During peak election hours, hundreds of students and watchers refresh live turnout and tally pages simultaneously.

- **Atomic File-Locked Cache (`LiveResultsCache.php`):** Instead of running expensive aggregate `COUNT`/`GROUP BY` queries against MariaDB on every page view, live election tallies and college turnout breakdowns are served through an atomic file-locked cache (`flock` + temp-file rename) with explicit invalidation on ballot submission and administrative state changes.
- **End-to-End Election Operations:** Comelec administrators manage election schedules, candidate slates, party lists, voter rosters, and real-time turnout monitoring from a dedicated control panel.

## Security, Accessibility & Regression Suite

- **Defense-in-Depth Auth:** Combines `bcrypt` credential hashing, hardened session regeneration, per-route CSRF token verification, and login rate-limiting.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA Accessibility:** Built with semantic HTML, keyboard-operable ballot controls, high-contrast status indicators, and responsive mobile-first layouts styled with **Tailwind CSS 4** and **vanilla JavaScript**.
- **Automated Verification:** Backed by an **18-script / 843-check regression suite** (`tests/run-all.php`) exercising ballot scoping, CSRF protection, cache invalidation, and administrative access boundaries.

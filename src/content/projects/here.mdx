## Problem & Origin

University student organizations and campus-wide assemblies regularly check in hundreds of students across gymnasiums, open courts, and lecture halls where cellular data is congested or unavailable. Manual sign-in sheets create long queues and transcription errors, while cloud-dependent biometric scanners stall whenever connectivity drops.

**Here!** began in 2023 as a college project built alongside Sheikha Encabo and Earlan Josh Sabillano. That original prototype proved that camera-based check-ins could replace paper rosters, but its architecture relied on sending captured frames to **AWS Rekognition** through **Firebase Cloud Functions**. Every check-in required a live internet round-trip and incurred per-invocation cloud vision costs—two constraints that made large-scale campus deployment impractical.

## The 2026 Ground-Up Rebuild

In 2026, I re-architected **Here!** from scratch so the entire recognition pipeline runs **100% on-device** inside a native Android application, paired with a static **Astro** web portal (`youre-here.vercel.app`) for public overview, privacy documentation, and APK distribution.

### On-Device Vision & Embedding Pipeline

- **Face Detection & Alignment:** Uses **Google ML Kit Face Detection** directly on the device camera stream to locate faces, verify framing, and crop aligned regions at interactive frame rates.
- **MobileFaceNet TFLite Embeddings:** Replaces cloud vision APIs with an embedded **MobileFaceNet TensorFlow Lite** model that extracts normalized facial feature embeddings locally on the phone—reducing per-scan cloud inference cost to zero.
- **Consent-First Face Data Lifecycle:** Students explicitly consent before enrolling facial embeddings, and raw enrollment imagery is never exposed to unauthorized roles.

### Offline-First Multi-Checker Sync

Campus events typically station multiple attendance checkers at different doors simultaneously, often without Wi-Fi.

- **Deterministic Composite Keys (`$uid~$timestamp`):** Attendance logs are written locally first via **Cloud Firestore** offline persistence using deterministic `$uid~$timestamp` composite identifiers, preventing key collisions when multiple checkers sync queued batches back to the cloud after an event.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Enforces distinct permissions for **Students**, **Attendance Checkers**, and **Administrators** across both the Flutter client and Firestore security rules.
- **Turnout Velocity & Export:** Organizers monitor real-time check-in velocity, late/absent breakdowns, and export verified CSV attendance sheets immediately after sync.

## Outcome

**Here!** is currently live in **Open Beta** for Android (`v1.0.0-beta.1`), transforming what started as a cloud-tethered classroom prototype into a resilient, zero-API-cost field tool for student organizations.
